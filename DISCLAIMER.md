Review the displayed information and report fork-specific problems to the [fork issue tracker](https://github.com/AngeloHague/alt1-job-gauges/issues).

## Hosted app and installation

Open the [hosted fork](https://alt1-job-gauges-fork.egglo.dev/) or use this ALT1 installation URL:

```text
alt1://addapp/https://alt1-job-gauges-fork.egglo.dev/appconfig.json
```

This installs the independently hosted fork, not the original upstream app.
